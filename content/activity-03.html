Practise inductive and deductive reasoning, Pólya's steps, problem-solving strategies, sequences and puzzles. Show your reasoning in each solution box.

Scoring and how to type answers

10 graded items, 1 point each, plus a short reflection. Write your solution in each solution box, then type your final answer or choose a letter. Right after submitting you will see your score and which items are correct or wrong.

  • Number answers: a decimal with at least 3 significant figures (0.618, 32.64) or an exact value (3/8, (1+sqrt(5))/2, 2^5). Do not type units, commas or the % sign; the unit is shown beside the box. Answers within 0.5% of the key are accepted unless the item says otherwise.
  • Multiple choice: choose one letter. A written solution is optional but recommended.
  • Formula answers: type the expression, e.g. 3n+2 or n^2+1. Use ^ for powers. Any correct equivalent form is accepted. The box below the answer shows how your answer was read.
